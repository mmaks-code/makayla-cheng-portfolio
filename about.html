About Me

Hi, I'm Makayla!

I'm originally from Los Angeles, California, and I'm currently a senior at the University of Pennsylvania, studying Bioengineering while simultaneously pursuing an accelerated master's degree in Robotics and a minor in Computer Science.

I'm passionate about developing innovative technologies that improve healthcare, particularly at the intersection of medical devices, mechanical design, and robotics. I enjoy combining hands-on prototyping, computational tools, and creative problem-solving to tackle challenging engineering problems.

Through my experiences in medical device R&D, academic research, and interdisciplinary engineering projects, I've developed a strong interest in translating ideas into practical solutions that can make a meaningful impact.
